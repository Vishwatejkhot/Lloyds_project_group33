Title: Efficient Commercial Bank Customer Credit Risk Assessment Based on LightGBM and Feature Engineering
Authors: Yanjie Sun, Zhike Gong, Quan Shi, Lin Chen
Year: 2023
Source: arXiv

Summary:
This paper focuses on credit risk assessment for commercial banks using LightGBM. The authors show that feature engineering, missing value handling, encoding, and imbalance treatment improve model performance. The paper is useful because our project also depends heavily on feature engineering.

Conclusion:
The study concludes that carefully engineered features can improve credit risk prediction. It also shows that banking models need strong preprocessing before model training.

Connection to Lloyds Project:
This supports our feature engineering work, especially mortgage ratios, compliance signals, maturity indicators, and sector-level features.
