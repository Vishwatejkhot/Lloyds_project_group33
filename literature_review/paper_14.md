Title: Exploring Explainable AI in the Financial Sector: Perspectives of Banks and Supervisory Authorities

Authors: Ouren Kuiper, Martin van den Berg, Joost van der Burgt, Stefan Leijnen
Year: 2022
Source: Springer

Summary:
This paper explores how banks and financial regulators view Explainable AI in practice. It discusses transparency, fairness, accountability, and the growing need for explainable AI in financial decision-making.

Conclusion:
The paper concludes that explainability is essential for trustworthy AI and that clear guidance is needed for financial institutions to implement explainable AI effectively.

Connection to Lloyds Project:
This paper supports the importance of using explainable machine learning models in financial applications such as our Lloyds project.
