Title: Interpretable Credit Default Prediction with Ensemble Learning and SHAP
Authors: Shiqi Yang, Ziyi Huang, Wengran Xiao, Xinyu Shen
Year: 2025
Source: arXiv

Summary:
This paper compares models such as Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, and LightGBM for credit default prediction. It uses SHAP to understand which features influence decisions. The paper is useful because it shows that ensemble models work well for imbalanced and complex credit datasets.

Conclusion:
The paper concludes that ensemble learning improves prediction performance, while SHAP improves interpretability. This makes the models more useful in real financial settings.
