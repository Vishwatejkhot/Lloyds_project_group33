Title: Finding the Sweet Spot: Optimal Data Augmentation Ratio for Imbalanced Credit Scoring Using ADASYN
Author: Luis H. Chia
Year: 2025
Source: arXiv

Summary:
This paper focuses on class imbalance in credit scoring. It explains that default cases are often rare, so models may become biased toward the majority class. The paper tests oversampling methods such as SMOTE and ADASYN and shows that fully balancing the data is not always best.

Conclusion:
The study concludes that imbalance handling must be done carefully. Over-sampling too much can reduce real-world performance.

Connection to Lloyds Project:
This is useful for our Risk Signal and Lending Need labels because they are imbalanced. It supports our use of class weighting and precision/recall/F1 instead of accuracy only
