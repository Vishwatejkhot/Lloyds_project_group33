Title: Beyond Accuracy in Artificial Intelligence Based Credit Scoring Systems
Authors: Marc Schmitt, Mark Cummins
Year: 2023
Source: SSRN

Summary:
The paper argues that machine learning systems in finance should not be evaluated solely based on predictive accuracy. It emphasizes explainability, sustainability, and trust in AI decision support systems.

Conclusion:
AI systems in banking must balance accuracy, transparency, and sustainability to achieve long-term adoption.

Connection to Lloyds Project:
Our project produces explainable outputs for commercial banking decisions. This paper supports the need for transparent AI systems in regulated environments.
