Title: Explainable Machine Learning in Credit Risk Management
Authors: Niklas Bussmann et al.
Year: 2021
Source: Springer

Summary:
This paper investigates the use of SHAP and explainable AI techniques in credit risk management. The authors demonstrate how machine learning models can provide transparent and trustworthy predictions while maintaining high performance.

Conclusion:
The study concludes that explainable AI is essential for modern banking applications and regulatory compliance.

Connection to Lloyds Project:
This paper directly supports our use of SHAP and XGBoost for SME lending and risk prediction. It reinforces the importance of transparent decision-making for relationship managers.
