Title: Explainable Artificial Intelligence Credit Risk Assessment using Machine Learning
Authors: Shreya, Harsh Pathak
Year: 2025
Source: arXiv

Summary:
This paper uses machine learning models such as XGBoost, LightGBM, and Random Forest for credit risk assessment. It also uses SHAP and LIME to explain model predictions. The paper is useful because it combines prediction accuracy with explainability, which is very important in banking.

Conclusion:
The paper shows that explainable machine learning can support transparent credit risk decisions. It also highlights the importance of using multiple evaluation metrics such as ROC-AUC, precision, recall, and F1-score.

Connection to Lloyds Project:
This directly connects to our project because we are also using machine learning and explainability to support commercial banking decisions. It supports our use of SHAP and tree-based models.
