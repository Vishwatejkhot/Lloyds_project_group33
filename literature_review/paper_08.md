Title: Performance and Interpretability Comparisons of Supervised Machine Learning Algorithms: An Empirical Study
Authors: Alice J. Liu, Arpita Mukherjee, Linwei Hu, Jie Chen, Vijayan N. Nair
Year: 2022
Source: arXiv

Summary:
This paper compares XGBoost, Random Forest, and neural networks using structured datasets. It studies both predictive performance and interpretability. The paper is useful because our project must balance performance with explainability.

Conclusion:
The paper shows that different algorithms have different strengths. XGBoost can perform well, but model interpretation must also be considered.

Connection to Lloyds Project:
This helps justify why we compare multiple models instead of using only one. It supports our model evaluation and interpretability strategy.
