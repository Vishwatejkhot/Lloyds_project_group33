Title: Artificial Intelligence (AI)-enabled Credit Scoring in Banking and FinTech: In Search of Maximum Prediction Accuracy

Authors: Marc Schmitt, Marc Roper
Year: 2023
Source: SSRN

Summary:
This paper evaluates several machine learning models for credit scoring and introduces stacking (super learning) to improve predictive accuracy. It compares Logistic Regression, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting and Deep Learning before combining them into an ensemble model.

Conclusion:
The study concludes that stacking multiple strong machine learning models can outperform individual classifiers and improve credit risk prediction performance.

Connection to Lloyds Project:
This paper supports our comparison of multiple machine learning models and highlights the value of ensemble methods for improving prediction accuracy.
