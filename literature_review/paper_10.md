Title: Explainable AI in Credit Risk Management

Authors: Branka Hadji Misheva, Ali Hirsa, Joerg Osterrieder, Onkar Kulkarni, Stephen Fung Lin
Year: 2021
Source: arXiv

Summary:
This paper investigates the use of Explainable AI (XAI) techniques in credit risk management. The authors apply both LIME and SHAP to machine learning credit scoring models and compare how these methods provide local and global explanations while maintaining predictive performance.

Conclusion:
The paper concludes that explainability techniques such as SHAP and LIME improve transparency and trust in credit scoring systems, making AI models more suitable for real-world financial applications.

Connection to Lloyds Project:
This paper is relevant because our project uses SHAP to explain machine learning predictions for growth opportunities and risk signals, helping make model decisions more interpretable.
