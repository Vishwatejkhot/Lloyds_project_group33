Paper: 9

Title: A Unified Approach to Interpreting Model Predictions
Authors: Scott Lundberg, Su-In Lee
Year: 2017
Source: arXiv / NeurIPS

Summary:
This is the original SHAP paper. It explains how SHAP gives each feature an importance value for a prediction. Although it is older than 2022, it is essential because many recent explainable AI papers are based on it.

Conclusion:
The paper concludes that SHAP provides a consistent and theoretically grounded way to explain model predictions.

Connection to Lloyds Project:
This is important because our project uses SHAP to explain why a company is predicted as a growth opportunity, risk signal, or lending prospect.
