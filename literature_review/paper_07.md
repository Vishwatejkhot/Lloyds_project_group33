Title: Why do tree-based models still outperform deep learning on tabular data?
Authors: Léo Grinsztajn, Edouard Oyallon, Gaël Varoquaux
Year: 2022
Source: arXiv

Summary:
This paper compares deep learning and tree-based models on tabular datasets. It finds that models such as Random Forest and XGBoost often perform better than deep learning for structured data. This is important because Companies House data is tabular.

Conclusion:
The paper concludes that tree-based models remain strong for tabular prediction tasks. They are also easier to train and interpret than many deep learning models.

Connection to Lloyds Project:
This supports our model choices: Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost. It gives academic justification for not using deep learning at this stage.
