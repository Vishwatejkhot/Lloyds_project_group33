Title: Enabling Machine Learning Algorithms for Credit Scoring – Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) Methods for Clear Understanding of Complex Predictive Models

Authors: Przemysław Biecek, Marcin Chlebus, Janusz Gajda, Alicja Gosiewska, Anna Kozak, Dominik Ogonowski, Jakub Sztachelski, Piotr Wojewnik
Year: 2021
Source: Research Paper

Summary:
This paper compares traditional credit scoring models with modern machine learning algorithms and demonstrates how explainable AI techniques improve the transparency of complex models used in lending decisions.

Conclusion:
The authors conclude that advanced tree-based models achieve higher predictive performance while XAI methods make these models understandable and practical for financial institutions.

Connection to Lloyds Project:
This paper supports our use of ensemble learning models and SHAP explanations to build accurate and explainable credit risk prediction models.
