Title: Algorithmic Bias in AI-Based Credit Scoring Systems: Financial Inclusion, Risk Modeling, and Ethical Constraints

Author: Chaitanya Kumar Gali
Year: 2025
Source: Research Paper

Summary:
This paper examines algorithmic bias in AI-based credit scoring systems. It discusses how machine learning models can improve prediction accuracy while also raising concerns about fairness, transparency, and financial inclusion.

Conclusion:
The paper concludes that responsible AI governance, transparent models, and fairness-aware practices are necessary to ensure ethical and reliable AI-based lending decisions.

Connection to Lloyds Project:
This paper is relevant because our project focuses on building reliable and explainable machine learning models for financial prediction while considering transparency and responsible AI practices.
